# Currencies

# Currencies

## 

## **Resource Economy Balance**

The resource system is designed around these principles:

1. **Core Loop Resources**: Shadow Essence flows freely, allowing regular pack purchases and basic activities.  
2. **Progression Resources**: Soul Shards are moderate scarcity, making leveling strategic but achievable.  
3. **Premium Resources**: Lunar Crystals are valuable but earnable through dedicated play  
4. **End-Game Resources**: Void Energy represents true scarcity, reserved for significant achievements.

# Soul Shards

## **Soul Shards**

**Primary Function**: Card leveling currency

**Acquisition Methods**:

* Earned from duplicate cards in packs  
* Rewards from expeditions  
* Daily and weekly quests  
* Special events  
* Achievements  
* Login rewards

**Used For**:

* Primary resource for leveling up cards  
* Major component in tier transition costs  
* Required for card Awakening  
* Required for card Transcendence

# Lunar Crystals

## **Lunar Crystals**

**Primary Function**: Premium currency for special features

**Acquisition Methods**:

* Purchased with real money  
* Earned rarely from high-level achievements  
* Special events  
* Daily login streaks (7-day, 30-day milestones)  
* Expedition rewards (rarely)

**Used For**:

* Purchasing premium card packs  
* Required for high-tier transitions (Midnight → Umbral → Eternal)  
* Necessary component for Awakening and Transcendence  
* Instantly completing expeditions  
* Purchasing special event access  
* Unlocking premium cosmetics  
* Buying missing resources

# Void Energy

## **Void Energy**

**Primary Function**: End-game resource for top-tier upgrades

**Acquisition Methods**:

* High-level expeditions (Void Nexus, etc.)  
* Cosmic events  
* Breaking down Eternal tier duplicate cards  
* End-game achievements  
* Special event rewards (rarely)

**Used For**:

* Required for Transcendence of high-tier cards  
* Unlocking special abilities on Eternal cards  
* Access to special high-level expeditions  
* Creating universal Soul Shards (usable on any card)

# Events

# Cosmic Cycles

**Twilight Cycle (6PM \- 6AM local time)**

* Shadow, Lycanthrope, Undead, Infernal types get \+30% generation  
* Beast types get \-10% generation  
* Special "Witching Hour" events at midnight (12-2AM)

**Solar Cycle (6AM \- 6PM local time)**

* Beast, Stone, Magic types get \+20% generation  
* Shadow, Lycanthrope, Undead types get \-10% generation  
* Special "Daybreak" events every morning (6-8AM)

**Lunar Cycle**

* Full Moon (based on real lunar calendar): All Lycanthrope cards get \+100% generation  
* New Moon (based on real lunar calendar): All Shadow and Curse cards get \+75% generation  
* Blood Moon (rare special event): All Blood cards get \+200% generation  
* Solar Eclipse (rare special event): All cards can use their special abilities without cooldown

**Void Cycles**  
A special end-game feature that provides unique daily bonuses and challenges:

* Monday: Beast and Lycanthrope day \- double expedition rewards  
* Tuesday: Magic and Fae day \- 50% faster collection times  
* Wednesday: Blood and Undead day \- double resource generation  
* Thursday: Shadow and Spirit day \- all abilities cost 50% less  
* Friday: Stone and Cursed day \- triple fusion effectiveness  
* Saturday: Infernal and Necromancy day \- special reward crates  
* Sunday: All types balanced \- random powerful bonuses
